Geospatial measurement

Draw a roof on a satellite image and get quantities that flow straight into an estimate.

role
Built
company
Granular.ai
stack
Leaflet, Mapbox, Turf.js, React
link
tool ↗

problem

Estimates start from measurements. Measuring by hand or in a separate tool means retyping numbers, and retyped numbers go wrong.

approach

  • Interactive polygon editing on satellite imagery with Leaflet and Mapbox.
  • Coordinate transformations and area calculations with Turf.js, so the area math is accurate rather than pixel-based.
  • The measured output feeds roof quantities directly into the estimate workflow, with no manual copy step.

result

One tool replaces a manual measuring step and powers the quantity engine behind Estimate V6. It is live in production.
