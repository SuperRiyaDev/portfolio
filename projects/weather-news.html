Weather news

A public page where anyone can look up radar and weather events for a date and an address.

role
Built
company
Granular.ai
stack
React, Leaflet, Mapbox, Turf.js, NEXRAD radar
link
live ↗

problem

Property and roofing questions often start with “what happened here, and when?” Answering that usually means digging through separate weather sources.

approach

  • NEXRAD radar layers shown on an interactive map.
  • Search by date and by address to jump to the right place and day.
  • Built on the same mapping stack as the measurement tool: Leaflet, Mapbox, and Turf.js.

result

Shipped as a public, live feature on the product site. I do not have usage numbers to quote.
