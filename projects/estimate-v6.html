Estimate V6

The core quoting product at Granular.ai: every edit is a revision, and a signed revision never changes.

role
Architected and built
company
Granular.ai
stack
TypeScript, React, Node.js, MongoDB, tRPC
scale
108 modules · 91 components · 54 tests

problem

Roofing quotes change after inspection, after vendor price updates, and after a homeowner asks for a different scope. Once a homeowner signs, the numbers have to stay exactly as signed, which matters for insurance and contractor workflows.

approach

  • Each job can hold several estimates. Every edit creates a numbered revision with builder, presentation, pricing, and audit snapshots.
  • Signing makes a revision immutable. Pricing is captured with a hash so totals cannot drift after approval.
  • Change orders fork a new revision instead of editing signed work.
  • A formula-driven quantity engine turns roof area, squares, ridge, hip and eave lengths, facets, and waste factor into priced line items.
  • Vendor catalogs and DocuSeal e-signature are integrated; vendor- specific materials stay separate from generic catalog items.

result

Shipped as the quoting core of the new CRM: 108 backend modules, 91 UI components, and 54 tests. Reusable templates are designed to cut setup by 15–30 minutes per job.
