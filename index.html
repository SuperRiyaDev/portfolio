about

I started as a mechanical engineer, not a programmer. What carried over is the habit I still work by: take a messy problem, break it down, and keep going until it works. I moved into software through a full-stack fellowship, and I have been building ever since.

I don’t attach myself to one language or framework. I pick up whatever the problem needs, whether that is a map library, a payments API, a Kubernetes manifest, or an LLM tool-calling loop, and I’m happy to be the person who reads the docs first. The problem decides the tech, not the other way round.

I enjoy the unglamorous part too: tracing a slow or broken system to its real cause, then leaving it clearer than I found it. I work remotely, and I’m looking for a team that cares about craft and likes people who figure things out.
